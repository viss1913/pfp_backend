# Platform Business Capabilities

The Antigravity PFP platform covers all major areas of individual financial well-being:

### 👵 Retirement Planning (PENSION)
- State pension estimation based on current IPK and future contributions.
- Integration of OPS (Accumulated Pension) capital into the active investment strategy.
- Gap analysis: showing the difference between desired and projected retirement income.

### 💰 Investment & Passive Income (RENT)
- Simulation of dividend/interest growth.
- Strategy selection based on Risk-Profile (Conservative to Aggressive).
- Calculation of required capital for a "Living on Interest" lifestyle.

### 🛡 Risk Management & Life Insurance (LIFE)
- Integration of Life Insurance (NSJ/ISJ) as a goal and a protective instrument.
- Calculation of tax refunds (Deductions) and co-financing benefits.

### 🏦 Liquid Capital & Cash Flow
- Visualizing the "Shared Pool" of liquid assets.
- Monthly cash flow breakdown showing survivors (investable surplus) and deficits.
- Smart replenishment logic for financial reserves.

### 🏠 Targeted Savings (OTHER)
- High-level modeling for real estate, education, or vehicle purchases.
- Time-to-buy estimation based on current savings rate.
