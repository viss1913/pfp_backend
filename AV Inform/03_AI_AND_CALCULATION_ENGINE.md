# Calculation Engine & AI Capabilities

## 🧠 The Intelligence Layer
The Antigravity platform is not just a calculator; it’s an intelligent advisor. We combine deterministic financial math with probabilistic AI reasoning.

### 1. Hybrid Calculation Approach
- **Deterministic Component**: Accurate multi-year simulations of compound interest, tax refunds, and state co-financing.
- **Probabilistic Component (AI)**: Analyzing client goals, risk profiles, and qualitative data to suggest optimal strategies.

### 2. Smart Allocation System
Our proprietary "Smart Allocation" logic solves the common problem of "Goal Conflict":
- It dynamically distributes one-time capital and monthly savings across all user goals.
- Prioritizes mandatory goals (like Financial Reserve or Retirement) while optimizing for high-yield investment goals.

### 3. AI-Powered Insights (Gemini Integration)
- **Automatic Summary Generation**: Translates rows of numbers into human-readable advice.
- **Interactive Financial Chat**: Agents can ask the AI to "Stress test the plan" or "Suggest a more aggressive strategy."
- **Prompt Engineering**: Highly tuned instructions that understand the specific nuances of the local financial market.

## 📊 Core Simulation Features
- **Inflation Modeling**: Dynamic indexing of both target amounts and expenses.
- **Tax Benefit Optimization**: Automated identification of the best routes for PDS and NSJ deductions.
- **Cash Flow Visualization**: Ready-to-use data structures for Bar and Pie charts, provided via the API.
